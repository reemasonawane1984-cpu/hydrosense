💧 HydroSense

📚 Water Safety Resources

Learn simple practices that can help communities monitor and manage water quality.

🧪

Water Quality Testing

  • Check important water-quality parameters regularly.
  • Record test results for future comparison.
  • Report unusual water-quality conditions.
🚰

Safe Water Storage

  • Keep water storage containers clean.
  • Keep containers covered.
  • Use clean utensils when handling stored water.
🧼

Water Hygiene

  • Keep hands and utensils clean.
  • Avoid contaminating stored drinking water.
  • Maintain cleanliness around water sources.
⚙️

RO Maintenance

  • Maintain RO units regularly.
  • Record service and maintenance activities.
  • Replace components according to appropriate maintenance guidance.
📢

Report Problems

  • Report unusual taste, smell, colour, or other concerns.
  • Mention the location of the problem.
  • Provide a clear description when submitting a complaint.
📊

Keep Records

  • Keep previous water test results.
  • Compare results over time.
  • Use records to identify recurring problems.
⚠️ Important: HydroSense is a prototype for water-quality monitoring and reporting. Its screening results should not be treated as laboratory certification or a substitute for appropriate professional testing.
